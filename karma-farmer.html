IN SHORT

The Karma Farmer app itself does not collect any personal data. We only receive personal data if you buy Glow through Google Play, use a test version through TestFlight or send us an email; what Apple and Google process under their own responsibility when you download the app, buy Glow or leave a rating is described below. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The app itself never connects to the internet, neither to us nor to anyone else. The only exceptions are buying Glow and the rating prompt: for these, the app talks to the App Store or Google Play through your device’s services. Everything you do and write in the app stays on your device, including your reflections. There is no automated decision-making and no profiling.

WHAT IS STORED ON YOUR DEVICE

  • Your Karma, your level, your streak and your longest streak
  • When you last completed a mission, how many you have completed in total and per category, plus your Karma history by day
  • The categories you chose, and a mission you swapped for the day
  • Your reflections: date, mission and the text you write yourself
  • Whether you want a reminder for your mission, and at what time
  • Whether Glow is unlocked, when you last used streak repair and mission swap, and whether you have already used the one free streak repair
  • Whether you have already gone through the introduction on first launch

This data is kept in a storage area that belongs to the app alone and that the operating system shields from other apps. We have no access to it, not even to your reflections. You can delete everything in the settings with “Reset progress”; only whether Glow is unlocked is kept, so that you keep your purchase. If you remove the app, the system deletes the data as well. Copies in backups are covered in the next section.

Storing it on your device, and the reading described below for the reminder and for Glow, are strictly necessary for the features you use (Section 25 (2) No. 2 TDDDG, the German law on data protection in telecommunications and digital services). Without it, your progress and your reflections would be gone the next time you open the app.

BACKUPS OF YOUR DEVICE

If you use your device’s backup, for example iCloud Backup on Apple devices or the backup to your Google account on Android, it may include this app’s data and restore it after a reinstall. You set up this backup yourself. It is processed by Apple or Google under their privacy policies, not by us.

DAILY REMINDER

The daily reminder is optional; the app works without it. If you turn it on, the app asks once for permission to send notifications and schedules the reminder on your device at the time you chose. So that it arrives at the right time, the app reads your device’s time zone at startup; that stays on your device too. No push service and no server are involved: the reminder is created on your device, and we do not learn whether or when you receive it. You can turn it off at any time in the app’s settings or in your device’s settings.

SHARING AND RATING

If you tap “Share”, the app opens your device’s share menu with a ready-made text about your mission or your streak. Where you send it is up to you; we learn nothing about it. The app never shares your reflections.

After your 10th, 30th and 60th completed mission, the app asks the system to show the rating prompt from Apple or Google; whether it appears is up to the system. If you tap “Rate app” in the settings, this prompt or the app’s page in the App Store or on Google Play opens. What you enter there goes to Apple or Google, not to us. Reviews you write in the store are visible to us just as they are to everyone else, and we can reply to them publicly.

BUYING GLOW

You buy Glow once. There is no subscription, and nothing renews. The purchase is handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play), and their privacy policies apply (apple.com/legal/privacy, policies.google.com/privacy). So that Glow comes back on its own on a new device or after you reinstall the app, the app checks at startup through your device’s purchase service whether you have already bought Glow. When you download the app, Apple or Google process data under their own responsibility. CE QUI EST ENREGISTRÉ SUR TON APPAREIL

  • Ton Karma, ton niveau, ta série et ta plus longue série
  • Le moment où tu as terminé ta dernière mission, combien tu en as terminé au total et par catégorie, ainsi que ton historique de Karma par jour
  • Les catégories que tu as choisies, et une mission du jour que tu as changée
  • Tes réflexions : date, mission et le texte que tu écris toi-même

PARTAGER ET NOTER
