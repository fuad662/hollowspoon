IN SHORT

The Mitrechner app does not collect any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. WHAT IS STORED ON YOUR DEVICE

  • Your lists with their names, items, quantities, prices and highlights
  • When a price was last entered in a list, as the date of the shopping trip
  • The budget of a list, if you set one
  • Completed shopping trips in your history, with their date
  • Whether Mitrechner Plus is unlocked

This data is kept in a storage area that belongs to the app alone and that the operating system shields from other apps. We have no access to it. You can delete it in the app, one by one or with “Clear”. If you remove the app, the system deletes it as well. Copies in backups are covered in the next section.

Storing it on your device is strictly necessary for the feature you are using (Section 25 (2) No. 2 TDDDG, the German law on data protection in telecommunications and digital services). Without it, your list would be empty the next time you open the app.

BACKUPS OF YOUR DEVICE

If you use your device’s backup, for example iCloud Backup on Apple devices or the backup to your Google account on Android, it may include this app’s data and restore it after a reinstall. You set up this backup yourself. It is processed by Apple or Google under their privacy policies, not by us.

BUYING MITRECHNER PLUS

The purchase is handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play), and their privacy policies apply (apple.com/legal/privacy, policies.google.com/privacy). The app only receives a confirmation of the purchase from the store and remembers on your device that Mitrechner Plus is unlocked. We do not learn your name or your payment details.

APP STORE AND GOOGLE PLAY

When you download the app, Apple or Google process data under their own responsibility. From Apple we only receive aggregated figures, such as how often the app was downloaded or bought, and crash reports if you have agreed in your device settings to share them with developers. They do not tell us who you are.

From Google we receive the same kind of figures and reports and, in addition, an order summary for each purchase of Mitrechner Plus: order number, date, product, price and country. For purchases in countries where we rather than Google are the seller, for example the United States or Mexico, the state or region and the postal code are added, because they are needed there for tax purposes. Reviews you write in the store are visible to us just as they are to everyone else, and we can reply to them publicly.

If you send us an email, we process your email address, your name if you give it, and the content of your message in order to reply.
