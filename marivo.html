IN SHORT

The Marivo app itself does not collect any personal data. We only receive personal data if you buy a song pack through Google Play or send us an email; what Apple and Google process under their own responsibility when you download the app or make a purchase is described below. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The app itself never connects to the internet, neither to us nor to anyone else. The only exceptions are purchases and restoring purchases: for these, the app talks to the App Store or Google Play through your device’s purchase service. Everything you do in the app stays on your device and, if you have set it up, in your device backup in your Apple or Google account; we have no access to it. There is no automated decision-making and no profiling.

WHAT IS STORED ON YOUR DEVICE

  • Your settings: music and effects volume, note speed, audio offset, note offset, whether vibration is on, the three visual effects, whether the melody drops out when you miss and whether automatic timing is on
  • Your best results per song and difficulty: score, accuracy, longest combo, rank and how often you played, plus the best results from earlier versions of the game
  • Your favorites, the song you played last, the difficulty you chose last and the filters of the song list
  • Whether you have played the tutorial
  • Which song packs are unlocked
  • A log of the last 20 songs you played, including ones you quit: which song on which difficulty, date and time, whether you finished it, your accuracy, how many notes you hit and how well, and whether your taps landed early or late on average. Plus technical measurements from the song: the refresh rate of your screen, how long individual frames took, the audio delay, your offset settings and the operating system. The log helps us look into timing and stutters on our own test devices; the app does not send it anywhere. It deletes older entries itself.

During the calibration test, the app measures when you tap along to the beat. Only the result, the delay in milliseconds, is stored. While you play, the app also measures whether your taps land early or late on average and evens that out next time, as long as automatic timing is on. Here too, only the correction in milliseconds is stored.

So that a crash does not cost you any progress, the app keeps backup copies of the same file on the device.

This data is kept in a storage area that belongs to the app alone and that the operating system shields from other apps. We have no access to it. If you remove the app, the system deletes it as well. Copies in backups are covered in the next section.

Storing it on your device is strictly necessary for the features you use (Section 25 (2) No. 2 TDDDG, the German law on data protection in telecommunications and digital services). Without it, your settings, best results and unlocked packs would be gone the next time you open the app.

On Android, your device’s Google backup may include this app’s game progress and restore it after a reinstall or on a new device, if you have set it up. Only the file with the data listed above and its backup copy are backed up, no caches. Google keeps the backup in a private area of your Google account, keeps only the most recent one and encrypts it end to end with your device’s PIN, pattern or password if you use Android 9 or later and a screen lock.
