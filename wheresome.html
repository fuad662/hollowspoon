The recommendation works entirely in flight mode. The app uses a network connection for three things: to make or restore the Full Access purchase, where the exchange is with Apple or Google and their privacy terms apply; to load a photo of the place found (see "Place photos"); and in the free version to load advertising (see "Advertising").

PLACE PHOTOS

For a place it has found, the app shows a photo from Pexels (operated by Canva Germany GmbH, Berlin) when there is one. For that the app sends the photo search, the place name and the country, to Pexels through a service we run at Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., USA); your IP address may technically be passed to Cloudflare in the process (cloudflare.com/privacypolicy). The photo found is downloaded from Pexels directly; Pexels receives your IP address and the request, nothing about you. Pexels' privacy policy applies (pexels.com/privacy-policy). The photographer and Pexels are named and linked right at the photo.

When you share a card, the image is produced on your device and handed to whichever app you choose in the system share sheet. The card carries the place, the country, the wishes you picked and typical climate figures. It carries no identifier, no invite code and nothing about you.

The place page has links to providers for hotels, holiday homes, flights and rental cars: Booking.com, Airbnb, Google Flights and Kayak. A link opens the provider's website in your browser. The address carries only the place name, the country and a travel window taken from the month you chose; the app sends nothing about you. From the moment it opens, that provider's privacy terms apply.

The app requests no location permission, no contacts access and no camera access. On iOS the free version may show the system's tracking permission question; it belongs to the advertising consent dialog, and you can decline it.
