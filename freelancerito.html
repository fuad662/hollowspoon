WHAT IS STORED ON YOUR DEVICE

  • Your settings: currency, language, appearance and whether you have seen the introduction
  • With Freelancerito Plus, your custom defaults and cost types
  • With Freelancerito Plus, your saved calculations: job name, date, currency, hourly value, your lines of effort and direct costs with their names, hours and amounts, safety buffer and the calculated prices
  • With Freelancerito Plus, your saved scenarios: offer price, discount, extra effort and date

Storing it on your device is strictly necessary for the feature you are using (Section 25 (2) No. 2 TDDDG, the German law on data protection in telecommunications and digital services). Without it, your calculations and settings would be gone the next time you open the app.

BACKUPS OF YOUR DEVICE

If you use your device’s backup, for example iCloud Backup on Apple devices or the backup to your Google account on Android, it may include this app’s data and restore it after a reinstall. You set up this backup yourself. It is processed by Apple or Google under their privacy policies, not by us.

SHARING AS PDF OR TEXT

With Freelancerito Plus you can share a calculation. For this, the app creates one of three versions on your device and opens the share menu with it:

  • the calculation as a PDF for you: job name, date, your lines of effort and costs with names, hours and amounts, hourly value, safety buffer, the three prices with how each comes about, and the prices you tested
  • the price overview as a PDF for your clients: job name, date, the names of your lines without hours or amounts, and one price, with a discount if there is one
  • a short text: job name, effort, direct costs and the three prices

The job name also appears in the file name and in the document properties of the PDF file. The PDF file you shared last stays in the app’s cache until you share the next one or the system clears the cache. You decide where it goes. What the app you choose in the share menu does with it is governed by that app’s privacy policy. Nothing reaches us in the process.
