IN SHORT

The Freelancerito app itself does not collect any personal data. We only receive personal data if you buy Freelancerito Plus through Google Play or send us an email; what Apple and Google process under their own responsibility when you download or buy is described below. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The app itself never connects to the internet, neither to us nor to anyone else. The only exception is buying Freelancerito Plus: for that, the app talks to the App Store or Google Play through your device’s purchase service. Everything you enter stays on your device. There is no automated decision-making and no profiling.

WHAT IS STORED ON YOUR DEVICE

  • Your settings: currency, language, appearance and whether you have seen the introduction
  • With Freelancerito Plus, your custom defaults and cost types
  • With Freelancerito Plus, your saved calculations: job name, date, currency, hourly value, effort, direct costs, safety buffer and the calculated prices
  • With Freelancerito Plus, your saved scenarios: offer price, discount, extra effort and date
  • Whether Freelancerito Plus is unlocked

What you are currently entering in the calculator is only kept while the app is running. A calculation is stored only when you save it yourself.

This data is kept in a storage area that belongs to the app alone and that the operating system shields from other apps. We have no access to it. You can delete saved calculations, scenarios and custom cost types in the app one by one. If you remove the app, the system deletes all of this data as well. Copies in backups are covered in the next section.

Storing it on your device is strictly necessary for the feature you are using (Section 25 (2) No. 2 TDDDG, the German law on data protection in telecommunications and digital services). Without it, your calculations and settings would be gone the next time you open the app.

BACKUPS OF YOUR DEVICE

If you use your device’s backup, for example iCloud Backup on Apple devices or the backup to your Google account on Android, it may include this app’s data and restore it after a reinstall. You set up this backup yourself. It is processed by Apple or Google under their privacy policies, not by us.

SHARING AS TEXT OR PDF

When you share a summary with Freelancerito Plus, the app creates it as text or as a PDF file on your device and opens the share menu with it. It contains the job name, the effort, the direct costs and the three prices. The PDF file you shared last stays in the app’s cache until you share the next one or the system clears the cache. You decide where the summary goes. What the app you choose in the share menu does with it is governed by that app’s privacy policy. Nothing reaches us in the process.

BUYING FREELANCERITO PLUS

The purchase is handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play), and their privacy policies apply (apple.com/legal/privacy, policies.google.com/privacy). Through your device’s purchase service, the app also asks the store for the price it shows before the purchase, and for your earlier purchases when you tap “Restore Purchases”. The app only receives a confirmation of the purchase from the store and remembers on your device that Freelancerito Plus is unlocked. We do not learn your name or your payment details.

APP STORE AND GOOGLE PLAY

When you download the app, Apple or Google process data under their own responsibility. From Apple we only receive aggregated figures, such as how often the app was downloaded or bought, and crash reports if you have agreed in your device settings to share them with developers. They do not tell us who you are.

From Google we receive the same kind of figures and reports and, in addition, an order summary for each purchase of Freelancerito Plus: order number, date, product, price and country. For purchases in countries where we rather than Google are the seller, for example the United States, the state or region and the postal code are added, because they are needed there for tax purposes. We do not receive your name, your email address or your payment details from Google either. We use this information for bookkeeping, taxes and refunds (Art. 6(1)(b) and (c) GDPR) and keep it for as long as German commercial and tax law requires (Section 147 AO, Section 257 HGB).

Reviews you write in the store are visible to us just as they are to everyone else, and we can reply to them publicly.
